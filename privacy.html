Privacy Policy

KeyLockPro2 • Last updated September 27, 2026

KeyLockPro2 is designed as a local-first password manager. Protecting your privacy is a core part of the app's design.

Information We Collect

KeyLockPro2 does not require an account and does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information for advertising or marketing.

Password and Vault Data

Your saved usernames, passwords, websites, notes, groups, and related vault information are stored on your device in an encrypted vault.

KeyLockPro2 does not operate a server that receives or stores the contents of your vault.

Encrypted Backups

If you choose to export a backup, KeyLockPro2 creates an encrypted backup file.

You control where that file is saved and are responsible for protecting the master password required to open it.

Biometrics

If you enable biometric unlocking, authentication is handled by Apple's system frameworks.

KeyLockPro2 does not receive or store your Face ID biometric data.

Clipboard

When you choose to copy credential information, it may temporarily be placed on the device clipboard so you can paste it into another app.

KeyLockPro2 is designed to request expiration of copied credential data after the clipboard timeout selected in the app.

Analytics and Advertising

KeyLockPro2 does not include advertising and is not designed to use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

KeyLockPro2 is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as KeyLockPro2 changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
