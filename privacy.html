Privacy Policy for KeyLockPro2

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Privacy First: KeyLockPro2 is designed to keep your passwords and vault data private on your device. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to the passwords, usernames, notes, or other credentials stored in your vault.

Information We Collect

KeyLockPro2 does not require you to create an account and does not require you to provide personal information to the developer in order to use the app.

Your vault may contain information that you choose to enter, including:

  • Account names
  • Usernames
  • Passwords
  • Website addresses
  • Notes
  • Groups and organizational information

This information is stored in your encrypted vault on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to the contents of your vault.

Vault Encryption

KeyLockPro2 encrypts your vault before it is stored on your device. Your Master Password is used as part of the process that protects access to your encrypted vault.

Your Master Password is not transmitted to or stored by the developer.

The developer cannot recover your Master Password. If you forget your Master Password and cannot access your vault using an enabled authentication method, your stored information may become permanently inaccessible.

Face ID and Biometric Authentication

If you choose to enable Face ID or another supported biometric authentication method, authentication is handled by Apple's operating system and security frameworks.

KeyLockPro2 does not receive or store your biometric data.

Encrypted Backups

KeyLockPro2 allows you to create encrypted backups of your vault. Backup files are created at your request and saved or shared using locations and services that you select.

The developer does not automatically receive copies of your backup files.

You are responsible for protecting your backup files and remembering the Master Password required to access them.

Clipboard

When you choose to copy information such as a password, KeyLockPro2 uses the device clipboard. The app is designed to use local-only clipboard behavior and automatically expire copied sensitive information after the configured period.

In-App Purchases

KeyLockPro2 can be downloaded and used for free with a limit of up to 10 saved passwords.

An optional one-time KeyLockPro2 Pro in-app purchase unlocks:

  • Unlimited password storage
  • Password Generator access

In-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive your complete payment card or Apple Account payment credentials.

KeyLockPro2 uses RevenueCat to help manage and verify in-app purchase entitlement status. Purchase-related technical information may therefore be processed by Apple and RevenueCat as necessary to provide purchase and restoration functionality.

Analytics and Advertising

KeyLockPro2 does not display third-party advertising.

The app does not sell the contents of your vault or use your saved passwords or credentials for advertising.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or trade the contents of your KeyLockPro2 vault.

Information may be processed by Apple or RevenueCat where necessary to provide App Store purchase and entitlement functionality. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

KeyLockPro2 is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The developer does not operate an account system that collects vault contents from users.

Security

KeyLockPro2 uses Apple's security technologies and modern cryptographic methods to protect locally stored vault information. However, no software or security system can guarantee absolute protection against every possible threat.

You should use a strong, unique Master Password and protect access to your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KeyLockPro2 features or services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
