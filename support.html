Free Version

KeyLockPro2 is free to download and allows you to save up to 10 passwords.

Your existing passwords remain available even if you have reached the free limit. Once the limit is reached, you will need KeyLockPro2 Pro before adding additional passwords.

KeyLockPro2 Pro

KeyLockPro2 Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase that unlocks:

  • Unlimited password storage
  • Password Generator

The Pro upgrade is a lifetime unlock and is not a subscription.

Restore Purchases

If you previously purchased KeyLockPro2 Pro but the app does not recognize your purchase, open KeyLockPro2 and use the Restore Purchases option.

Make sure the device is signed into the Apple Account associated with the original purchase.

Forgotten Master Password

Your Master Password cannot be recovered by the developer.

KeyLockPro2 does not send your Master Password to the developer and does not maintain a copy that can be used to reset it.

If you lose access to your Master Password and cannot authenticate using an enabled authentication method, you may need to erase the local vault and start over.

Face ID

If Face ID is enabled, KeyLockPro2 can use Apple's biometric authentication system to make unlocking your vault more convenient.

Biometric authentication is controlled by iOS. KeyLockPro2 does not receive or store your facial recognition data.

Encrypted Backups

KeyLockPro2 supports encrypted vault backups.

Keep backup files somewhere safe. You will need the appropriate Master Password to access an encrypted backup.

The developer cannot decrypt your backup or recover a forgotten Master Password.

Password Generator

The Password Generator is included with KeyLockPro2 Pro and can generate strong passwords using combinations of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.

Reporting a Problem

When contacting support, please include:

  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • Your KeyLockPro2 app version
  • A description of what happened
  • The steps that caused the problem, if known

Do not email your Master Password or any passwords stored in your vault.
